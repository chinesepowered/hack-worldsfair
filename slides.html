2 / 4 · the product

sotto: paid quietly, proven loudly.

confidential · not anonymous

The same payment, three viewpoints. Amounts are encrypted on-chain; the owner sees everything; an auditor with a viewing key recovers the exact figure from the ledger — trusting nothing from the agent.

Three rails. Two protocols. One fetch.

Solana · confidential

Token-2022 confidential transfer, range proof staged in an SPL Record account, payment id in a memo.

payee/auditor: find the validity proof the transfer references → decrypt lo + (hi ≪ 16)

Zcash · shielded

A shielded note whose encrypted memo is the payment id. Amount, sender and receiver all hidden.

payee: viewing-key settler scans for memo == id, value ≥ price

Tempo · MPP

Stripe's payments chain, spoken natively through the Machine Payments Protocol. Public, bounded, receipted.
